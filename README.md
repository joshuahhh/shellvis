### ⚠️ ⛔ 🚫 WARNING 🚫 ⛔ ⚠️

Although the ostensible goal of ShellVis is to provide a safe sandboxed environment where shell programmers can play and explore and frolic freely in the verdant meadows of Bash without fear of disaster, we did not entirely acheive this goal, as described in §6 of [our paper](https://joshuahhh.com/shellvis/shellvis.pdf). Specifically: absolute references will not be sandboxed; `rm -rf /` will run on your real root directory.

It would be great to fix this someday, hypothetically. (Lots of things would be great, hypothetically.) Until then, act with caution and do not be lulled into a false sense of safety.

With that out of the way, here's the readme!

# ShellVis: Sandboxed Live Programming for Shell Scripts

Please see [our website](https://joshuahhh.com/shellvis) for paper and other details.
